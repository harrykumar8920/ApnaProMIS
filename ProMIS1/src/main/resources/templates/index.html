About ProMIS

SFIO is a multi-disciplinary organization under Ministry of Corporate Affairs, consisting of experts in the field of accountancy, forensic auditing, law, information technology, investigation, company law, capital market and taxation for detecting and prosecuting or recommending for prosecution white-collar crimes/ frauds. SFIO has head office in New Delhi and regional offices in Maharashtra, Telengana, Tamil Nadu and West Bengal.

SFIO deals with prosecution of cases as assigned by MCA. The prosecution division handles the cases investigated by SFIO after receipt of instruction from MCA. The division is involved in drafting of criminal complaints/petitions/ applications/counter affidavits/ replies/ appeals/ etc. to be filed return various Courts/ Forums. Moreover, Typically the criminal complaint filed by SFIO involve large number of accused both individual as well as companies. Once the cognizance of complaint is taken the litigation increases multiple times.

Similarly the sanction received from the Ministry of Corporate Affairs as the investigation report consists of instruction which also includes proceedings to be instituted at NCLT with regards to disgorgement as well renewal of auditing, management takeover, winding etc. Which also involves multiple respondents with multiple jurisdictions. Moreover complaints are to be filed before Disciplinary Committee of ICAI/ICSI/NFRA. Litigation involves provision of Companies Act 1956 and Companies Act 2013 carrying forward of the legacy cases.

To overcome the problems in manual system, a new system is envisaged to provide facility to generate summons through workflow-based system which comprises of proper approval mechanisms.

ProMIS(Prosecution Management Information System) designed and developed by NIC in collaboration with domain experts from SFIO. This system enables SFIO in effective management of cases dealt by prosecution Unit. The system was built based on the workflow of SFIO. System assists in capturing information related to cases, companies and individuals thru user-friendly screens and generating periodic reports that are to be submitted to MCA. Once the prosecution of the case is taken up, it can be entered into the system. Typically SFIO plays Petitioner/Respondent/Appellant role in each case. At any point of time, the officers can view the case details and its progress thru dashboards provided.
